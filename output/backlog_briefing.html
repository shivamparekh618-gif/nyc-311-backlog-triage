Where to focus

Focus: Street Light Condition Growing

The backlog went from 183 to 295 open in 4 weeks, and 72% of it is past the 10-day target.

Reports held at about 72/week, but closures fell from 79 to 46/week starting the week of Jun 29. The demand didn't change. Capacity did.

If nothing changes: about 407 open in 4 weeks. Getting back to 183 within 8 weeks takes about 86 closures/week.

Next step: find out what changed the week of Jun 29 before moving anyone.

Plan for: Sidewalk Condition Chronic

48% of 244 open requests are past the 30-day target. Closures cover only 75% of new reports lately, and 68% across the whole period.

No single event caused this, so a short-term crew move won't fix it. It needs a capacity or policy decision: more sustained capacity, or triage by severity with an honest target.

Next step: bring this to the monthly planning review, not the weekly stand-up.

No action: Street Condition Healthy

In the week of May 11 this was the biggest backlog on the board: 330 open after reports spiked. Crews brought it back down to 29 with no intervention, and it now closes more than it receives.

Worth noting: the process already absorbs short spikes. The team shouldn't react to one bad week.
